## Film direction
**Palette:** 60% canvas (cream paper-anchor reads through mesh veil) / 30% hairline dashed rules + repeated canvas (no surface token; soft-editorial layers with translucency, not a second fill) / 10% accent — brand-primary (warm gold) carries hero words, CTA pill, and stat emphasis; brand-secondary (deep navy) reserved for ink and the brand-reveal scene's display type. Two-color limit per scene per preset rules. Ink stays brand-secondary navy throughout.
**Type:** display (serif) = hero claims and scene anchors only; body (sans) = supporting copy, location labels, list items; mono = eyebrow metadata and chapter marks. Italic `<em>` is the system's only emphasis — no uppercase anywhere (soft-editorial voice register). Sentence case throughout.
**Motion:** entries `EASE.entry`; heroes heavy; idle `EASE.drift`; exits none — scenes hold the final frame. Budget: one root-level camera move per scene (slow drift or dolly) + at most one breathing hero element; everything else rests. Stagger totals capped at 500ms.
**Ambient:** brand-color mesh background (heavy veil, brand-primary gold + brand-secondary navy + brand-accent as three subtle atmospheric blobs, reading as a warm restrained base at viewing distance) — consistent across all scenes including closer. Sparse float particles in brand-primary at low opacity as the only coexisting layer.
**Never (film-wide):** no drop shadows, no glow halos, no blur halos, no neon, no bokeh, no mesh gradients beyond the declared mesh default, no purple-blue AI gradient, no cards stacked on cards, no uppercase text, no dark ground scenes.
**Transitions:** `crossfade` + `push-slide` as primary vocabulary; `zoom-through` reserved for the finale only.
**Stillness-before-climax:** scenes 8 and 11 only.
**Assets:** no `assetCandidates` provided in any scene — this is a text-driven film. Every scene relies on typographic composition, brand color, and component structures for visual density.

# Voice register: soft-editorial

## From the site (DNA)
- Tone: warm
- Heading style: Sentence case

## Transform recipe

Take the brand's product description / value prop. Transform with:

1. Keep complete sentences. Sentence case throughout — soft-editorial reads
   UPPERCASE as shouting, so on-screen text stays sentence case everywhere.
2. Prefer one warm noun phrase over a list of features. The mood is "essayist's
   summary", not "product spec".
3. Drop hyperbole and superlatives (`best`, `most`, `revolutionary`). Replace with
   measured adjectives (`considered`, `quiet`, `careful`).
4. Use italic `<em>` inside a headline to highlight a single warm phrase — usually
   2–4 words mid-sentence. The italic is the system's emphasis.
5. End scenes with a quiet declarative — no exclamation, no question mark.
6. Cite proofs (years, customers, awards) as factual asides in italic serif at
   marker size, not as boastful pull-out stats.

**Example:**

- IN: `Figma helps teams design products collaboratively in real time`
- OUT: `A canvas where teams design <em>together</em>, in real time. — Trusted by four million, quietly.`

> Phase 4b scene workers: apply to DOM text only (headline / chip / button copy).
> Phase 2 narrator scripts are TTS-bound — do NOT uppercase or strip articles.
