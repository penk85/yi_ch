Young Israel of Cherry Hill — Your Family Belongs Here Cherry Hill is a vibrant community filled with growth and prosperity. Money Magazine named Cherry Hill one of the "Best Places to Live" in the United States. We are conveniently located only 15 minutes from Philadelphia, 1 hour from Lakewood and Atlantic City, 1.5 hours from New York City, and 2 hours from Baltimore. This warm and friendly South Jersey suburban community is ideal for Jewish family life: - Affordable housing ($300,000 - $580,000) - Excellent Jewish Day Schools and Yeshivas - Orthodox shuls with fantastic programming - An abundance of young families - Eruv, Mikvaos, Kosher establishments - Extensive Torah learning opportunities including a community kollel - Jewish Community Center, beautiful parks, nearby malls Young Israel of Cherry Hill is an orthodox synagogue on the West side of Cherry Hill, servicing the community since 1996. With a strong love for Torah values, Young Israel enriches the lives of the Jewish community with thought-provoking shiurim, unbelievable programming and a warm, welcoming atmosphere. With the leadership of Rabbi Jonathan Bienenfeld, we are united in the service of Hashem. We are one congregation, part of one people, serving one G-d b'simcha and b'ahavah. Kulanu K'echad — We Are All One. We are: - An incubator for spiritual growth - A shul that is fun and engaging - A community that feels like a family Come home to Cherry Hill. Visit yicherryhill.com
